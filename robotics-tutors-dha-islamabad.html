Sense, Decide, Act: The Three-Step Loop Inside Most Robots

Almost every school robot follows the same loop: sense the world, decide what to do, act on the decision — then repeat.

  • Sense: ultrasonic distance, line sensor, button, camera
  • Decide: if distance < 10 cm, turn; else go forward
  • Act: motors, LEDs, speakers

If the robot behaves oddly, check which stage failed: bad sensor reading, wrong rule, or motor not wired. Students who only tweak motor speed without checking the sensor often chase the wrong problem.
