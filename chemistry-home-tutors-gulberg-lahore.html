What Happens to Atoms During a Chemical Reaction

In a chemical reaction, atoms are rearranged into new combinations. Bonds break and form; atoms are not created or destroyed. That is why balanced equations matter: the same atoms must appear on both sides.

Physical changes (melting ice) keep the same substance. Chemical changes produce different substances with different properties — rust is not the same as shiny iron.
