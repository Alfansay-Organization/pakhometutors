Study Focus: Building Confidence in Physics Numericals

Many students understand textbook definitions but lose marks on multi-step numerical questions. A practical approach is to revise the formula sheet for the current chapter, solve a few guided examples with the tutor, then attempt mixed past-paper questions without looking at the solution first. Short, regular practice sessions often work better than long, infrequent ones—especially in the months before O Level, A Level or FSc exams.
