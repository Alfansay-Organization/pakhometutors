How to Check an Answer in 30 Seconds Before Moving On

Finishing early is useful only if the time is spent checking. A random re-read of the whole paper is slow. A 30-second check per numerical answer catches many slips.

Quick checks by type

  • Arithmetic: reverse the last operation (if you multiplied, divide once to verify)
  • Units: does the unit match what was asked?
  • Size: is the answer roughly sensible (speed of a person cannot be 300 m/s)?
  • Substitution: plug the answer back into the original equation

Example

You found x = 5 for 3x − 4 = 11. Check: 3(5) − 4 = 15 − 4 = 11. Confirmed in seconds.

Build the habit on homework so it is automatic in exams. Tutors supporting students in I-11 Islamabad can require a written check line under every final answer during practice.
