The Chemistry of Rusting Iron

Rusting is an electrochemical process: iron oxidises in the presence of both oxygen and water. The brown flakes are hydrated iron oxides.

  • Iron loses electrons (oxidation)
  • Oxygen gains electrons in the presence of water (reduction)
  • Moisture acts as a medium for ion movement

Paint and oil block air and water. Galvanising coats iron with zinc, which oxidises preferentially.
