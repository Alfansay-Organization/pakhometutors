Both options are useful, and the better choice depends on the student and the subject.

Home Tuition

A tutor visits the student’s home in DHA. This suits younger children, parents who want to observe the first few sessions, and subjects that benefit from face-to-face explanation and written practice.

Online tutors remove travel constraints between phases and can connect students with subject specialists who may not live near DHA. Online tuition works well for Cambridge subjects, coding and older students who are comfortable with digital tools.
