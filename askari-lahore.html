How Soap Removes Grease

Grease does not dissolve in water. Soap molecules have a water-loving end and a grease-loving end. They surround oil droplets, forming micelles that stay suspended in water so rinsing carries the dirt away.

That is emulsification — not magic, and not the same as a simple acid–base reaction.
