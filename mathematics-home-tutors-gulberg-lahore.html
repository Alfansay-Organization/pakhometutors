Why Negative Numbers Actually Make Sense

Negative numbers are not “less than nothing” in a mystical way. They extend the number line so subtraction always has an answer and so we can model opposites: debt vs credit, below zero vs above zero, left vs right.

If the temperature drops 5° from 2°, you need −3°. If you owe 10 more after already owing 5, you need −15. The rules for multiplying and adding negatives follow from keeping arithmetic consistent.
