Terminal Velocity: Why a Falling Object Eventually Stops Speeding Up

In free fall in a vacuum, speed keeps rising. In air, drag grows with speed until it balances weight. Net force becomes zero and acceleration stops — that steady speed is terminal velocity.

Force balance

Weight downward = drag (+ upthrust) upward. No unbalanced force → constant velocity, not zero velocity.

What changes terminal speed

  • Heavier object (same shape) → higher terminal speed
  • Larger area or more open shape → lower terminal speed
  • Denser fluid → stronger drag → lower terminal speed
