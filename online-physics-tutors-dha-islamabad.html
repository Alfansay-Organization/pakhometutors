Units: The Fastest Check on Every Physics Numerical

Before celebrating a number, ask: does the unit match the quantity? Speed in m/s, force in N, energy in J. If your algebra produced kg for a force, something broke.

Three habits

  1. Write the unit next to every given value
  2. Convert early (cm → m, g → kg) so formulae stay consistent
  3. Track units through the steps; cancel what cancels

Online lessons make this easy to enforce: keep units in the shared workspace for every line, not only the final answer.
