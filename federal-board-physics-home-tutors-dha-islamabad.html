Mass vs Weight: Stopping the Most Common Numerical Mix-Up

Mass is the amount of matter (kg). Weight is the gravitational force on that mass (N). Weight = mg. On the Moon g is smaller, so the same mass has less weight.

Exam traps

  • Using kg as if it were a force unit
  • Forgetting to multiply by g when a force equation needs newtons
  • Thinking mass changes when you go to the Moon

A spring balance measures weight (force). A beam balance compares masses and gives the same reading on Earth or Moon for the same object pair.
