Why Friction Can Be Both Useful and Harmful

Friction opposes sliding between surfaces. Without it, walking and car tyres would fail. With too much of the wrong kind, engines waste energy and parts wear out.

Useful: grip of shoes, brakes, matches
Harmful: heat and wear in machines, energy loss

Engineers reduce unwanted friction with lubricants and bearings; they increase useful friction with tread and rough surfaces.
