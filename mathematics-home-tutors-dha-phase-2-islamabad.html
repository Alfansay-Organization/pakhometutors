The Mathematics Behind Compound Interest

Simple interest grows in a straight line. Compound interest grows on a growing base — interest earns interest.

Core formula: A = P(1 + r)^n

Example: Rs 10,000 at 10% per year for 3 years compounds to Rs 13,310. Simple interest would give only Rs 13,000. The extra Rs 310 is interest on interest.
