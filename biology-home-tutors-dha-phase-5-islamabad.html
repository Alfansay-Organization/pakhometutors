Why Delivering Oxygen Needs Several Body Systems Working Together

Breathing alone is not enough. Oxygen must enter the blood, travel to tissues, and be used in cells. That requires the respiratory system, the circulatory system, and cellular respiration working as a chain.

The chain

  1. Lungs: gas exchange (O₂ in, CO₂ out)
  2. Blood and heart: transport
  3. Cells: use O₂ to release energy from food

A break at any link limits energy supply even if the other links are healthy.
