Two-Column Notes: Examples on the Left, Rules on the Right

Linear notes often bury the rule under a wall of examples. A two-column page makes the rule visible and testable.

How to set the page

  • Left column: one worked example, written fully
  • Right column: the general rule or steps in your own words

Example (solving equations)

Left: 2x + 3 = 11 → 2x = 8 → x = 4

Right: Undo operations in reverse order; do the same to both sides; check by substitution.

How to revise from the page

Cover the right column. Look only at the example. Say the rule out loud. Then cover the left and invent a new example that fits the rule.

This turns notes into a quiz tool instead of a re-reading script. Students with home tuition in I-10 Islamabad can build one two-column page at the end of each tuition session.
