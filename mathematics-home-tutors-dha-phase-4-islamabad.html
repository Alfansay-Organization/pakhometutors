The Hidden Pattern Behind Quadratic Equations

A quadratic is not a random collection of terms. The shape is always a parabola, and factorising, completing the square, and the formula are different routes to the same roots.

Standard form: ax² + bx + c = 0 (a ≠ 0)

Example: x² − 5x + 6 = 0 → (x − 2)(x − 3) = 0 → x = 2 or 3. The graph crosses at 2 and 3; the vertex sits halfway at x = 2.5.
