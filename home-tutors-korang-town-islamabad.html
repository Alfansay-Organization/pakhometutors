How Plants Transport Water from Roots to Leaves

Water enters roots, moves through xylem, and reaches leaves. The main upward pull comes from transpiration: water evaporating from leaves creates tension that draws a continuous water column upward (cohesion–tension idea).

Root pressure can contribute, especially at night, but for tall plants the transpiration pull dominates during the day.
