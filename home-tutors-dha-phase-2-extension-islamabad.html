Break Word Problems into Known, Find, and Bridge

Word problems overwhelm students when every sentence feels equally important. Sorting information into three labels turns the story into a solvable structure.

The three labels

  • Known: numbers, conditions, and facts given
  • Find: exactly what the question demands
  • Bridge: the rule, formula, or idea that connects Known to Find

Example

“A shop offers 20% off a Rs 2,500 bag. What is the sale price?”

Known: original = 2500; discount rate = 20%
Find: sale price
Bridge: sale price = original − (20% of original)

0.20 × 2500 = 500; 2500 − 500 = Rs 2,000

Write the three labels before calculating. Students in DHA Phase 2 Extension Islamabad can use this template on every word problem until it becomes automatic.
