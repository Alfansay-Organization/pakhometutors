How Oxygen Travels from the Lungs to the Body

Oxygen enters alveoli, diffuses into blood, binds mostly to haemoglobin in red blood cells, and is pumped by the heart through arteries to tissues. There it leaves the blood and enters cells for respiration.

Key steps

  1. Breathing moves air to the lungs
  2. Diffusion across the alveolar membrane
  3. Transport mainly via haemoglobin
  4. Delivery to cells; CO₂ returns for exhalation
