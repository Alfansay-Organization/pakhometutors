How to Build a Weekly Revision Timetable That Survives Real School Days

Perfect timetables fail when they ignore travel, fatigue, and family time. A useful plan is slightly shorter than you hope — and actually followed.

Design rules

  • Block school hours and sleep first; study only in what remains
  • Assign weak subjects to your best focus windows (often late afternoon, not midnight)
  • Keep two “buffer” slots empty for overflow or rest
  • Limit each block to 25–40 minutes with a clear task, not “do Chemistry”

Example evening block

4:30–5:00 — five mixed Maths questions (cold)
5:00–5:10 — mark and note one gap
5:10–5:30 — free / snack
5:30–6:00 — one English paragraph with evidence underlines
