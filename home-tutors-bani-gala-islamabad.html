Why Pressure Increases Underwater

Pressure in a fluid increases with depth because a deeper point supports the weight of more fluid above it. In water, roughly every 10 m of depth adds about one atmosphere of pressure.

Formula idea

P = ρgh — density × gravity × depth. Greater depth → greater pressure. That is why deep-sea structures need strength and why your ears feel change when you dive in a pool.
