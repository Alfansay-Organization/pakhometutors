Chemical Equilibrium Is Dynamic — Not a Reaction That Has Stopped

At equilibrium, concentrations of reactants and products stay constant — but molecules still react. Forward and reverse rates are equal, so there is no net change.

Consequences

  • Adding a reactant shifts the system to consume some of it (Le Chatelier)
  • A closed system is assumed for classic equilibrium arguments
  • Catalysts speed both directions equally; they do not change the equilibrium position

Writing a double arrow ⇌ is a reminder that both processes continue.
