Why Ice Floats on Water (And Why That Matters for Lakes)

Most solids sink in their liquid form. Ice is less dense than liquid water because of its open hexagonal crystal structure, so it floats.

Density = mass ÷ volume. When water freezes, molecules occupy more volume → lower density. Floating ice insulates lakes in winter. If ice sank, lakes could freeze from the bottom up.
