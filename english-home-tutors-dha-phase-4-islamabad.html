Why Evidence Must Be Explained — Not Only Quoted

Dropping a quotation into a paragraph does not earn analysis marks by itself. Examiners look for what you make of the evidence.

A reliable sequence: make a point → short quotation → explain the words (tone, implication, technique) → tie back to the question.

Example: Point — the writer presents the city as exhausting. Evidence — “the streets never slept.” Explanation — personification suggests constant noise, leaving no rest for residents.
