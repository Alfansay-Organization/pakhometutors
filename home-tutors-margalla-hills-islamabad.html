What Actually Happens When Iron Rusts

Rust is mainly iron oxide formed when iron reacts with oxygen in the presence of water. It is a redox process: iron is oxidised; oxygen is reduced. Salt water speeds the process by improving ionic conduction.

Rusting weakens structures because solid metal becomes flaky oxide. Prevention methods (paint, galvanising, alloys) block oxygen, water, or both.
