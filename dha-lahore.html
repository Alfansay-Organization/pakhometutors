How Seatbelts Demonstrate Newton’s Laws

In a crash or hard brake, the car slows because forces act on it. Your body keeps its forward velocity until something applies a force. A seatbelt stretches and pulls on you so you slow with the vehicle instead of continuing into the dashboard.

Newton links

  • First law: you keep moving until a force acts
  • Second law: a larger force (or longer stretch) changes your momentum more gently
  • Third law: you pull on the belt as the belt pulls on you
