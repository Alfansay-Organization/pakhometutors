Invent a Five-Question Mini-Test from Any Textbook Page

Waiting for a teacher’s quiz delays feedback. Any dense page can become a mini-test if you write the questions yourself.

Five question types to invent

  1. Define or state a key term in your own words
  2. Solve a numerical or short application
  3. Explain why a step or fact is true
  4. Spot the mistake in a wrong statement you invent
  5. Connect the idea to a real-life example

Answer on a blank sheet, then check against the book. The questions you could not write cleanly reveal weaker understanding than the ones you answered.

Do this twice a week on weak pages only. Families using home tutors in DHA Phase 4 Islamabad can share the five questions with the tutor as a ready-made starter for the next class.
