Why Metal Feels Colder Than Wood

Room-temperature metal and wood are often at the same temperature. Metal feels colder because it conducts heat away from your skin faster. Your hand loses heat quickly; nerves interpret that as “cold.” Wood conducts poorly, so less heat leaves your skin quickly.

Conductivity, not temperature difference alone, drives the sensation.
