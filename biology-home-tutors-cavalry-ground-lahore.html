How Digestion Actually Breaks Food Down

Digestion is both mechanical (chewing, churning) and chemical (enzymes). Large molecules become small enough to absorb: starch → sugars, proteins → amino acids, fats → fatty acids and glycerol.

Rough path

  • Mouth: chewing + amylase on starch
  • Stomach: acid + protease start on protein
  • Small intestine: major enzyme work + absorption
