How Leaf Structure Matches the Job of Photosynthesis

A leaf is not a random green sheet. Each major feature supports photosynthesis or gas exchange.

  • Broad flat shape: large surface for light
  • Thin blade: short diffusion path for CO₂
  • Palisade cells packed with chloroplasts: capture light
  • Stomata: let CO₂ in and O₂ out (with water vapour trade-off)
  • Veins: water in, sugars out

Linking structure to function turns a labelled diagram into an explanation question answer.
