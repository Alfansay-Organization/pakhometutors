Study Focus: Closing Algebra Gaps Before Exams

Algebra underpins much of O Level, Matric and A Level Mathematics. Students who rush into past papers without fixing equation skills often repeat the same mistakes. A useful plan is to list the algebra skills required for the next exam chapter, practise short mixed sets daily, and only then move to full past-paper questions. Mention "algebra first" or "exam papers first" when you contact us so the tutor priority is clear from day one.
