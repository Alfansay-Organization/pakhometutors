Why We Need Sleep

Sleep is not idle time. The brain consolidates memories, clears metabolic waste, and restores attention systems. Without enough sleep, focus drops, reaction time slows, and learning from the day sticks less well.

Student-relevant effects

  • Poor sleep → weaker recall in next-day tests
  • All-nighters often cost more marks than they gain
  • Consistent bedtimes support better attention in tuition and class
