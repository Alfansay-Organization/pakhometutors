How a Topic Sentence Controls an Entire Paragraph

A paragraph without a clear topic sentence wanders. The topic sentence states the paragraph’s one main claim; every other sentence should support, explain, or exemplify that claim.

Pattern that works in exams

  1. Topic sentence — the point
  2. Explanation — what you mean
  3. Evidence — quote, fact, or example
  4. Link — why the evidence matters

Weak: “Pollution is bad and also traffic is noisy.” Strong: “Industrial smoke is the main cause of poor air quality in the area described.”
