How Language Models Predict the Next Word

Large language models do not look up a single stored answer for every question. They estimate which token (word piece) is likely to come next given the text so far — over and over — to form a reply.

That is why small prompt changes can shift the answer, and why models can sound confident even when wrong: fluent next-word prediction is not the same as checked truth.

Student habit

Use AI output as a draft. Verify facts, formulas, and code by running or cross-checking. Ask for steps, then try the steps yourself without looking.
